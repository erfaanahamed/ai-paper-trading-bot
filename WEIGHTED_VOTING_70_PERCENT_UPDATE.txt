AI Brain V7 — Weighted Voting Threshold Update

Changes:
- BUY/SELL entries now require at least two strategies to agree.
- At least 70% of the active directional weighted votes must support the same side.
- Support is calculated from BUY + SELL votes that actually emitted a signal; silent strategies no longer dilute the displayed decision threshold.
- If neither side meets the threshold, the decision is WAIT.

This is a code change, not evidence of profitability. Test on historical data and paper trading before relying on it. The live Streamlit deployment has not been verified.
