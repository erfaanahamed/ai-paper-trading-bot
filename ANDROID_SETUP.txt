BRAIN AI TRADING BOT V2 — ANDROID QUICK GUIDE

1. Download and extract the ZIP.
2. Create a GitHub repository from your phone browser.
3. Upload app.py, requirements.txt, README.md, and this guide.
4. Open https://share.streamlit.io and deploy the repository with app.py as the entry point.
5. Open the app URL on Android Chrome. Chrome menu > Add to Home screen creates an app-like shortcut.

Dashboard features:
- One table scans every configured symbol.
- Trade History shows one row per trade, open/close details, entry/stop/targets, exit reason, virtual P&L, filters, and CSV download.
- AI Win Estimate % is a model estimate; Paper Win Rate % is calculated only from completed simulated trades and may be blank initially.
- Automatic paper trading scans every listed symbol using the selected entry timeframe.
- Daily paper-trading loss limit is 3%; risk budget is 0.5% per trade; max 20 new paper trades/day across the account.
- TP1 closes 60%, then remaining stop moves to break-even + buffer; TP2 closes the remaining 40%.

This is a paper-trading research prototype, not an APK and not a real-money broker bot. It needs the page/session to stay active to refresh, and cloud hosting may sleep or lose local files. Market data symbols can be proxies and may differ from a broker's CFD quotes. Local history files may be lost if cloud hosting restarts; download the CSV regularly or configure persistent storage.
